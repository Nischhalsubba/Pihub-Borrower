# Public PiHub product grounding used by Borrower

Reviewed against public pages: 2026-08-28.

This implementation is intentionally grounded in PiHub's public product positioning, not invented lender-software theatre.

- PiHub publicly describes a digital business-loan process and says it supports customers from loan search and application through implementation and ongoing loan management. That supports extending the Borrower product beyond closing into servicing.
- PiHub publicly describes corporate finance purposes such as acquisitions, recapitalizations, refinancing and growth, plus real-estate/infrastructure financing. Product discovery and application data therefore support multiple financing purposes and structures rather than a single mortgage form.
- Public PiHub case studies describe bridge/project/collateral-based financing, including a €3.7m mixed-use financing example. The funded demo facility is illustrative and clearly labelled demo, not represented as a live customer record.
- PiHub's public data-protection page describes rights including access, portability, correction, deletion and restriction under applicable circumstances. The Borrower UI exposes those as requests to Admin/Compliance, never as an instant destructive browser action.
- PiHub publishes a regulatory page. The software therefore treats auditability, permissions, durable record ownership and compliance boundaries as product requirements rather than decorative badges.

Public marketing claims do not define internal credit policy, legal advice, KYB rules, lender decisions or contractual servicing calculations. Those require confirmed internal requirements and production backend data.
